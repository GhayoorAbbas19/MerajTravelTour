Patentino muletto

Forklift licence, without the paperwork headache

The forklift licence (patentino muletto / carrello elevatore) is required by law to work in Italian warehouses, logistics and factories. We book your course, guide you through the exam and deliver the certificate.

  • Enrolment in an accredited course near you
  • Theory and practical modules as required by Accordo Stato-Regioni
  • Certificate valid across Italy, renewal reminders included
  • Support in Italian, English, Urdu and Punjabi
Get the licence

Who needs it?

Anyone operating a forklift at work in Italy: warehouse operators, logistics staff, factory workers and agency workers. Employers ask for it before your first shift, so get it ready in advance.
